# Product

**What:** Investigation Engine. Ask a question that needs several documents; it searches ~62k
records, follows evidence across them, and returns a report where every sentence is checked
against its sources, contradictions are shown, and missing information is said out loud.

**Who:** an engineer or incident manager after an outage; for this project also the Upgaming
reviewers judging the stage-2 task.

**Surface mode:** Operate (app UI). The tool disappears into the task.

**Pinned brief (from Saba, 2026-10-26):** ChatGPT-style, super minimalistic and simple, white
tone. Left sidebar with "New investigation", past investigations, and Documents / Search /
Evaluation at the bottom; the main area is the conversation with the input at the bottom.

**Assumptions (not confirmed):** light only (the brief says white); system font stack is
acceptable for product UI; English UI copy.
